

This is what the DJ sees. You can approve or reject songs as well as lock any more requests to give you a break; ideal for busy times, when food is being served at a wedding or something else. You can download a CSV at the end of the night too!

This is where users can enter their song requests; either by a touch screen (if you have one) or by scanning a QR code. It is designed for touch screens but can also be manually input via a standard QWERTY keyboard too. The search is linked to known iTunes songs, but you can type any song you want. You can also send the DJ a funny Emoji which flashes up on their screen for a few seconds, send a hug, a fire, a heart... or a poop!
